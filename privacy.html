1 What We Collect

When you use UPSC Study Tracker, we collect only what's necessary:

  • Email address — required for account creation
  • Name — optional, for personalization
  • Password — securely hashed, never stored in plain text
  • Study data — sessions, syllabus, notes, tasks, goals you enter
  • Payment info — only order/payment IDs from Razorpay (no card details)

2 How We Use It

We use your information only to:

  • Provide the study tracking service
  • Authenticate your account and secure your session
  • Save and sync your data across devices
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Send password reset emails when requested

🚫 We never sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with other users.

3 Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase — a secure, enterprise-grade platform. We use:

  • Row Level Security — you can only access your own data
  • HTTPS encryption — all traffic encrypted end-to-end
  • Encrypted storage — data encrypted at rest
  • Secure authentication — industry-standard JWT tokens

4 Payments

All payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive only order IDs, payment IDs, and status. We do not have access to your card, UPI, or bank credentials.

5 Cookies & Local Storage

We use minimal client-side storage:

  • Session tokens — to keep you logged in
  • Theme preference — dark/light mode
  • Timer state — to persist running timers across refreshes

🍪 We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Ever.

6 Your Rights

  • Access your data — export as JSON from Settings
  • Correct your data — edit anytime in the app
  • Delete your data — delete account from Settings

All data is permanently removed within 30 days of account deletion.

7 Children's Privacy

UPSC Study Tracker is intended for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date will change and material changes will be announced in-app.
