About UPSC Study Tracker

A study tracking platform built for serious UPSC aspirants — by someone who's been through the grind.

Our Story

UPSC preparation is brutal. Not because the syllabus is hard — but because staying consistent, tracking progress, and managing revisions across 2-3 years of preparation is nearly impossible without a system.

I tried Notion. It took 3 days to setup and 3 weeks to abandon. I tried paid UPSC apps. ₹500-1000/month for features I didn't need. I tried Excel sheets. Forgotten in a week.

So I built what I actually wanted — a simple, focused, UPSC-specific study tracker. No bloat, no distraction. Just: pick subject, hit timer, track progress.

💡 Our Mission

Give every UPSC aspirant — regardless of city, coaching, or budget — the tools that serious toppers use. Data-driven preparation shouldn't be a luxury.

What We Offer

  • Study Timer — Stopwatch, countdown, and Pomodoro with UPSC subjects pre-loaded
  • Complete Syllabus Tracker — GS Papers I-IV, Essay, CSAT, Optional
  • Spaced Repetition Revision — Auto-schedules 1/3/7/14/30/60 day cycles
  • PYQ Analytics — Year-wise, subject-wise tracking with accuracy %
  • Productivity Insights — Best study hours, neglected subjects, consistency
  • Mock Test Analytics — Score progression, weak areas
  • Goals, Tasks, Notes, Current Affairs — All in one place
