The Backstory

UPSC preparation is brutal. Not because the syllabus is hard — but because staying consistent, tracking progress, and managing revisions across 2–3 years of prep is nearly impossible without a system.

I tried Notion. It took 3 days to set up and 3 weeks to abandon. I tried paid UPSC apps. ₹500–1000/month for features I didn't need. I tried Excel sheets. Forgotten in a week.

So I built what I actually wanted — a simple, focused, UPSC-specific study tracker. No bloat, no distraction. Just: pick subject → hit timer → track progress.

🎯

Our Mission

Give every UPSC aspirant — regardless of city, coaching, or budget — the same tools that serious toppers use. Data-driven preparation shouldn't be a luxury.
