Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Is the app really free?
Yes. All core features (timer, syllabus, revision, PYQ tracker, notes, current affairs) free hain. Advanced analytics aur premium features ke liye optional paid plan hai.
Data kahan store hota hai? Safe hai?
Aapka data Supabase ke secure servers pe encrypted form mein store hota hai. Sirf aap apna data dekh sakte hain — koi aur access nahi kar sakta.
Phone pe kaam karta hai?
Bilkul. Website fully responsive hai — phone, tablet, desktop sab pe perfectly kaam karta hai. Mobile pe touch-friendly controls milte hain.
Payment kaise karein?
Razorpay ke through secure payments. UPI, card, netbanking sab support karte hain. Monthly plan ke liye UPI Autopay setup karna zaroori hai.
Cancel kaise karein agar mann badal jaye?
Koi commitment nahi. Monthly plan aap kabhi bhi cancel kar sakte hain — next billing cycle se koi charge nahi hoga. Yearly plan one-time payment hai.
Kya mera data delete ho sakta hai?
Kabhi bhi Settings se apna saara data export kar sakte hain. Ya aap chahein toh account delete bhi kar sakte hain — pura data permanently remove ho jayega.
Notion se better kyu hai?
Notion me tumhe khud templates banana padta hai, formulas likhni padti hain, aur track karna mushkil hota hai. Yahan complete UPSC syllabus pre-loaded hai, revision auto-schedule hoti hai, aur zero setup chahiye.
