Frequently Asked

Is the app really free?
Yes. All core features (timer, syllabus, revision, PYQ tracker, notes, current affairs) are free. Advanced analytics and premium features have an optional paid plan.
Where is my data stored? Is it safe?
Your data is stored encrypted on Supabase's secure servers. Only you can see your data — no one else can access it.
Does it work on mobile?
Absolutely. The website is fully responsive — it works perfectly on phone, tablet, and desktop. You get touch-friendly controls on mobile.
How do I make a payment?
Secure payments through Razorpay. We support UPI, cards, and netbanking.
How do I cancel if I change my mind?
No commitment. You can cancel your monthly plan anytime — no charges from the next billing cycle. The yearly plan is a one-time payment.
Can my data be deleted?
You can export all your data anytime from Settings. Or if you wish, you can delete your account — all data will be permanently removed.
Why is this better than Notion?
With Notion you have to build your own templates, write formulas, and tracking becomes difficult. Here the complete UPSC syllabus is pre-loaded, revisions auto-schedule, and zero setup is needed.
