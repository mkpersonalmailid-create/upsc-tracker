Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about the app, pricing, data privacy, and more.

How does "Study with Partner" work?
Set your optional + year. We match you with aspirants on the same track. Once both accept, see aggregate stats and chat daily. Your personal notes stay private.
Is the app really free?
Yes. All core features (timer, syllabus, revision, PYQ tracker, notes) are free forever. Study with Partner and analytics have an optional paid plan.
Where is my data stored? Is it safe?
Encrypted on Supabase with Row Level Security. Only you can see your data. Partner comparison shows only aggregate stats.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. Fully responsive, works on phone, tablet, and desktop. Installable as a PWA.
Can I have multiple partners?
Yes. Add multiple partners via Discover or email. Each gets their own comparison dashboard and separate chat.
How do I make a payment?
Secure payments through Razorpay. UPI, cards, netbanking supported.
Can my data be deleted?
Yes. Export all data anytime as JSON or CSV, or delete your account permanently.
