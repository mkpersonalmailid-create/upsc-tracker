Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about the app, pricing, data privacy, and more. Still stuck? Reach out on the contact page.

Is the app really free?
Yes. All core features (timer, syllabus, revision, PYQ tracker, notes, current affairs) are free forever. Advanced analytics and premium features have an optional paid plan.
Where is my data stored? Is it safe?
Your data is stored encrypted on Supabase's secure servers with Row Level Security. Only you can see your data — no one else, including us, can access it.
Does it work on mobile?
Absolutely. The website is fully responsive — it works perfectly on phone, tablet, and desktop. You get touch-friendly controls on mobile and it's installable as a PWA.
How do I make a payment?
Secure payments through Razorpay. We support UPI, credit/debit cards, and netbanking. All transactions are encrypted and PCI-compliant.
How do I cancel if I change my mind?
No commitment. You can cancel your monthly plan anytime — no charges from the next billing cycle. The yearly plan is a one-time payment with no auto-renewal.
Can my data be deleted?
Yes, absolutely. You can export all your data anytime from Settings as JSON or CSV. Or delete your entire account — all data will be permanently removed within 30 days.
Why is this better than Notion?
With Notion you have to build your own templates, write formulas, and tracking becomes difficult. Here the complete UPSC syllabus is pre-loaded, revisions auto-schedule, and zero setup is needed. Just login and start.
